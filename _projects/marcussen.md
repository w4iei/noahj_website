**→ [Open the dashboard: klavecimbel.com/marcussen](https://klavecimbel.com/marcussen/)**

When an organ is refurbished, an expert can hear which pipes have changed.
Hans Fidom, head of research at the Orgelpark, wants a model that listens the way he does, starting with the Marcussen organ in Rotterdam's Laurenskerk.
I built this dashboard to test which audio models agree with his ears.
