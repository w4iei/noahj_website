Musical source-separation systems are usually scored with objective metrics such as SDR.
This study asked whether those scores agree with what people actually hear.

Listeners rated separated audio in MUSHRA tests, the ITU-R method also used to grade codec artifacts.
I compared the metrics with the listeners' ratings using statistical analysis, including rank-based and pairwise comparisons.

## Paper

- N. Jaffe and J. A. Burgoyne, "Musical Source Separation Bake-Off: Comparing Objective Metrics with Human Perception," _IEEE WASPAA 2025_. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06917) · [PDF](/assets/pdf/Metrics_Bake_off_WASPAA_2025.pdf)
